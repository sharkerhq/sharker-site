Production-minded architecture

Start with visibility. Keep production in control.

Observer mode is designed as a passive monitoring layer. It does not block, rewrite or approve production SQL. Enforcement capabilities are a separate product direction, not a prerequisite for monitoring.

01

Connect

Define monitored database sources and collection boundaries.

02

Observe

Collect supported activity and operational telemetry.

03

Analyze

Review anomalies, risk signals and historical patterns.

04

Act

Investigate findings and apply changes through existing operational controls.

Engine coverage and deployment architecture are confirmed during technical scoping. Monitoring is not a substitute for access control, backup, DLP or a SIEM.
